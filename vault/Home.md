## Reading the graph

Colours are live search queries, so the graph is a market view rather than decoration:

| colour | meaning |
|---|---|
| green | company currently Strong Buy on the summary gauge |
| red | currently Strong Sell |
| amber | thin float — risk overrides posture here |
| teal | sector hubs |
| violet | signal prediction notes |
| slate | daily journal entries |

Set the local graph to **depth 2** (its settings cog) — depth 1 shows a company and its sector, depth 2 shows the sector's other members, which is the comparison worth having.
